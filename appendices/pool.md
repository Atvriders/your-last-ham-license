## Appendix A: The Complete 2024–2028 Amateur Extra Question Pool

This appendix is the complete 2024–2028 Amateur Extra question pool: all 599 active questions, exactly as the NCVEC Question Pool Committee published them, each with its four choices and the keyed answer. The NCVEC QPC released this pool into the public domain — "The NCVEC Question Pool Committee hereby releases into public domain the 2024-2028 Element 4 Extra Class Question Pool" — so it is reproduced here verbatim, wording and punctuation included. **It is valid for every Amateur Extra exam given from 2024-07-01 through 2028-06-30 — this pool expires on 2028-06-30.** The next Extra pool arrives for the exam cycle beginning 2028-07-01; when it does, the swap is contained — the new pool is ingested, this appendix and the chapters' Exam Focus picks are refreshed against it, and the teaching chapters stand. The text here already incorporates all four errata issued for this pool — 2024-01-31, 2024-11-08, 2025-09-24, and 2026-02-04 — with no fifth as of 2026-07-30. Where the General pool needed nine withdrawals over its life, this one has needed only four: E9E10, E2A13, E6D07, and E4D05 simply do not appear, exactly as the official document omits them — they were withdrawn without renumbering, so a few groups skip a number, but no active question is missing.

How to use it: every group of questions maps to one chapter of this book — read that chapter first, then come back and drill its questions here. Each entry shows the question and choices verbatim, then the correct answer with a one-line plain-language why naming the chapter that teaches it. Beneath each entry, the Published ID line carries the question's official answer letter and Part 97 rule reference exactly as printed in the pool document — including the original's small printing quirks, preserved rather than repaired: E1A06's superseded citation `[97.303(h)(1)]` (the 60-meter channel rules now live at §97.303(h)(3)), E1E10's `[97.509(m)]` and E1E11's `[97.509(i)]` (themselves errata corrections, printed as the current document carries them), the interior space in E1C10's choice A (`A. - 43 dB`), the double spaces in E2D04's choice D and E8B04's question stem, and the three figure-stem typos — E7G02's "the circuit in E7-3", E7G07's "Figure E73", and E9B04's "Figure E92".

The exam itself draws 50 questions from this pool — one from each of the 50 groups — and 37 correct answers pass. (You may see that quoted as "74%"; the rule pins the count — 37 of 50 — and the percentage is derived arithmetic.) Twenty-eight questions refer to the pool's ten official figures; all ten are redrawn here as original diagrams, embedded with the questions that use them. When you are ready to test yourself, the book's practice-test page draws a valid 50-question practice exam — one question per group — from this same pool.

> **E1A01** Why is it not legal to transmit a 3 kHz bandwidth USB signal with a carrier frequency of 14.348 MHz?
> A. USB is not used on 20-meter phone
> B. The lower 1 kHz of the signal is outside the 20-meter band
> C. 14.348 MHz is outside the 20-meter band
> D. The upper 1 kHz of the signal is outside the 20-meter band
> **Answer: D** — USB occupies carrier to carrier-plus-bandwidth, so 14.348 MHz puts energy to 14.351 — the upper 1 kHz leaves the band — taught in chapter 1.

Published ID line: `E1A01 (D) [97.305, 97.307(b)]`
